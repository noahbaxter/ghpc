Scene frames close at PsRnd::EndDrawing, not at vblank. 2026-09-21.

Symptom: flashing under --native, reported by hand play.

Metric, declared before the run: presents with no camera (blank) and presents
under 80% of their neighbours' draw median (short), both toward 0. The change
cannot move repeat/skip on game_screen if the guest already runs at 60.

One binary, three route.sh arms, 85s each, full career loop to the second
song. The vblank arm is GHPC_SCENE_BOUNDARY=vblank, the old boundary.

    vblank  presents=4929 repeat=0.0%  skip=0.2% blank=754 short=4.5%
    guest   presents=5018 repeat=13.8% skip=0.0% blank=21  short=0.3%
    guest2  presents=5017 repeat=13.8% skip=0.0% blank=20  short=0.3%

Both arms reached the same screens in the same order and game rate held
1.00. Blank presents were exactly the zero-draw presents in the vblank arm:
a vblank that landed between two guest frames published an empty one.

Per screen, guest arm:

    game_screen     n=1785 repeat=1   blank=0  short=0
    loading_screen  n=349  repeat=79  blank=0  short=0
    boot (none)     n=606  repeat=543 blank=21 short=0
    lose_screen     n=129  repeat=4   blank=0  short=14

The repeats are the presenter re-showing the last frame while the guest draws
nothing through the seam (boot, loading, menu transitions), which is what the
PS2 does with its display buffer. The 21 blanks are boot, before any guest
frame. vblank still closes a frame when EndDrawing has not run for 8 vblanks
and something was drawn, so a screen that never reaches EndDrawing publishes.
