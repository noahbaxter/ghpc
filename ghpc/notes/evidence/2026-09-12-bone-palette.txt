The object transform is a bone palette, not a matrix. 2026-09-12, release.

The last round measured the vertex transform to 0.56 px in x and left a 4 px
dy it blamed on a stale object matrix. That diagnosis was wrong. Gameplay
meshes are skinned, the transform is per-vertex, and once it is applied the
residual is zero to GS subpixel.

WHERE THE MATRIX COMES FROM, read from the ELF at 0x43f038.

PsMesh::DrawShowing branches at 0x43f098 on mBones (this+0x13c):

  mBones == 0  -> 0x43f2b8. Header 0x6C0402A4, V4-32 NUM 4 ADDR 0x2a4 = 676.
                  0x43f2ec calls WorldXfm(this+0x40) and the 0x43f308 loop
                  copies its four quadwords in.
  mBones != 0  -> 0x43f0a4. ONE header 0x6C140294: V4-32, NUM 0x14 = 20
                  quadwords, ADDR 0x294 = 660. It spans qw660..679, and the
                  cursor advances 0x140, the same 20 quadwords. Bone matrices
                  0..3 land at $s1+0x00/0x40/0x80/0xc0 = qw660..675, each
                  Multiply(bone.xfm, WorldXfm(bone.obj)). qw676..679 is
                  $s1+0x100, filled by one of two blocks:
                    0x43f24c, when any of mBones+0x14/+0x20/+0x2c is set:
                      rows (1 0 0) (0 1 0) (0 0 1) and sq $zero at +0x130.
                      Exact identity.
                    0x43f294, only bone 0 in use: copies $s1+0x00..0x30 to
                      $s1+0x100..0x130, so qw676 is bone 0's matrix.

Both skinned blocks branch straight to 0x43f330 and never run 0x43f2ec. That
is the whole of last round's "reached DrawFaces without passing 0x43f2ec".
There was no missing writer to find. 0x43f434 is also not a second call site:
it is the only jal DrawFaces in the function, sitting in a per-material loop
(bnez $16, 0x43f370 at 0x43f43c) that all three paths fall into.

THE WEIGHTS were already in the log and had been read as colours. RndMesh::Vert
carries four floats at +0x20, named Hmx::Color in the decomp. On every gameplay
vert they sum to 1.0 and take shapes like (0 0 1 0), (0 0.5 0.5 0), (1 0 0 0).
They are the four bone weights, indexing the palette in order.

THE TRANSFORM, confirmed:

    world = sum over b of weight[b] * (pos * Bone[b])    b = 0..3
    clip  = world * M            M = qw700..703 as rows, implied pos.w of 1
    q     = 1 / clip.w
    x     = clip.x * q * qw696.x + qw697.x
    y     = clip.y * q * qw696.y + qw697.y
    s,t   = uv * q

Bone[b] rows 0..2 are the Matrix3 and row 3 the translation, the same shape a
Transform has. The w lane of each row is stale packet content (167.903, the
camera's M row 3 w) because the stores are 3-component.

RESIDUAL, six meshes on the first gameplay frame, 171 unambiguous pairs.
Pairs are object verts against submitted verts, keyed on uv recovered as st/q
because fst=0, keeping only matches 10x closer than their runner-up so the uv
aliasing that widened last round's ranges is excluded rather than averaged in.

  mesh        pairs   identity qw676        owner +0xa0          bone palette
  0x007efce0   21   dx +1.11 dy  +7.61   dx -0.48 dy -4.04   dx +0.04 dy +0.03
  0x007ef830   30   dx -3.66 dy  +5.17   dx -5.12 dy -6.52   dx +0.04 dy +0.04

  0x007f1220   31   dx +8.67 dy +10.52   dx +6.98 dy -1.88   dx +0.03 dy +0.03

Medians, in GS pixels. The bone-palette column's full range is [0.00, 0.07] on
both axes for all six meshes, and q lands at -0.000%. One sixteenth of a pixel
is 0.0625, so the whole residual is the GS subpixel quantisation. Nothing is
left over.

Both rigid candidates are worse than the table's medians suggest, because
their error is not constant: the identity and +0xa0 ranges span 8 to 40 px
within a single mesh. A rigid matrix cannot fit a weighted blend, and the 4 px
dy was never evidence of staleness. It was the median of that spread on the
one mesh that got measured.

This also settles an open question in rnd-seam.md: skinning runs on VU1, not
the EE, and the backend's vertex contract needs the weights and the palette,
not just pos/norm/uv.

WHAT THE PRE-DECLARED METRIC SAID. The round predicted that qw676 would be the
real matrix and that using it would take dy sub-pixel. qw676 is real and was
captured from the packet, but using it made dy worse, +8.2 against the +0xa0
arm's -3.5 on the sample mesh. The hypothesis failed on its own metric. The
reason it failed is what closed the problem.

CAPTURE. ghpcCaptureObjXfm in the DrawShowing override, at label_43f330 where
all three paths converge and before either register dies ($s2 in the delay
slot at 0x43f338, $s1 at 0x43f344). It reads mBones and the three bone slots
to name the path, then copies qw660..679 out of the packet the guest just
built. It recomputes nothing, so it cannot manufacture agreement.
