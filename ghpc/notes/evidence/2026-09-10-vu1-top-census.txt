VU1 runaway split by TOP and by input freshness, 2026-09-10, build-debug, 300s
GHPC_COUNTIN=0.5 GHPC_VU1_CENSUS=2000, bootskip on, game_screen at t=60.8

PROBE

[vu1/topcensus] keys every VU1 run by (startPc, TOP as XTOP returns it) and
by whether qw[TOP] was last written by an UNPACK since the previous MSCAL
(fresh) or not (stale). Freshness is captured at MSCAL issue from
g_ghpcLastWriter, before the program's own stores land. [vu1/topin] prints
qw[TOP] at issue for the first 40 cut-offs, every 500th after, and the first
two ended runs per key.

Q1: IS TOP THE VARIABLE? NO.

  pc0x30b0/top0    fresh ebit=190  cut=28
  pc0x30b0/top330  fresh ebit=1    cut=0
  pc0xcd8/top0     fresh ebit=21367 cut=6595
  pc0xcd8/top330   fresh ebit=4913 cut=1
  pc0xcd8/top514   fresh ebit=870  cut=538
  pc0xcd8/top515   fresh ebit=219  cut=134
  pc0xcd8/top610   fresh ebit=8814 cut=6943
  (total=52000, end of run)

TOP=0 runs end normally most of the time, and gameplay cut-offs sit on TOPs
of 514, 515 and 610 as well. Those three are not halves of the BASE=0
OFFSET=330 double buffer at all.

Q2: WAS THE INPUT DELIVERED? YES, ALWAYS.

stale = 0 in every bucket across 52000 runs. Every cut-off got a qw[TOP]
unpacked since the previous MSCAL. The VIF delivered something; the question
is what.

THREE POPULATIONS, NOT ONE

A. menus, 0xcd8, rare (mscal 3363, 4601, 5716, 6699): the freshly unpacked
   header is all zeros.
B. loading_screen, 0x30b0, from mscal 7655: header identical to healthy runs
   (00000004 00000011 00000012 00000614). Preceded at mscal 7649 by
   BASE 0 -> 0 and OFFSET 330 -> 0 with num=3, which turns double buffering
   off.
C. game_screen, 0xcd8, the bulk: preceded at mscal 8392 by one packet parsing
   ~20 OFFSET VIFcodes 28 to 32 bytes apart with num 100 to 155 and immediates
   604 to 664. Those are payload words read as VIFcodes. They set OFFSET to
   junk, which is where TOP 514/515/610 come from.

THE DESYNC BEHIND C

[vif1] TOTALS at end of run:

  chunks=37380 truncUnpack=186 bytesDiscarded=431040
  dirtyChunks=193 dirtyAfterTrunc=186 firstBadAtPos0=187 invalidOps=118798

Early in the run (before gameplay): truncUnpack=0, dirtyChunks=4. The first
UNPACK truncations appear at log line 22030, right after game_screen (21997),
and the OFFSET flood follows at 22368. 186 of 193 dirty chunks come right after
a truncated UNPACK. processVIF1Data drops a command whose payload runs past the
chunk end, carries nothing, and the next chunk parses from mid-payload.

boot-sequence-reference.md already names this as "a second, smaller defect"
(25 per 45s at boot). At gameplay it is the dominant desync.

1305 OFFSET VIFcodes over the run. Invalid opcodes 0x37 to 0x43 total ~118k.

THE FIX: CARRY A STRADDLING COMMAND INTO THE NEXT CHUNK

processVIF1Data now keeps a command whose payload runs past the chunk end
(UNPACK, MPG, DIRECT, STMASK, STROW, STCOL), from its VIFcode on, in
m_vif1Residual and parses it ahead of the next chunk. Cleared on VIF1 reset.
GHPC_VIF1_NO_RESIDUAL=1 restores the old drop, for a same-build control.

Mechanism, build-debug, 300s, same probes, game_screen at t=60.8:

                    before (drop)   after (carry)
  carried                 -             247
  truncUnpack           186               0
  dirtyAfterTrunc       186               0
  dirtyChunks           193               6
  invalidOps         118798            1018
  OFFSET VIFcodes      1305              75
  0xcd8 cut share      ~27%            8.4%

The "before" column is the run above, on a build that differs only by this
change.

Speed, release build, same binary, 300s each, GHPC_COUNTIN=0.5:

  fix on    SAME rung 9  eerate 0.7%  2.15 Mcycles/sec  fps 0.35  menus 85.3%
  fix off   SAME rung 9  eerate 0.7%  2.13 Mcycles/sec  fps 0.26  menus 85.1%

Verdict SAME. The desync was real and is gone, but it was not what holds
gameplay speed down. After the fix, cut-off runs still retire 336M of 510M VU1
instructions (66%), from 5426 of 64960 0xcd8 runs. The runaway survives a
clean stream.

Cut-offs after the fix, by TOP:

  pc0xcd8/top0    ebit=44761 cut=2602
  pc0xcd8/top514  ebit=1836  cut=1141
  pc0xcd8/top515  ebit=2280  cut=1675
  pc0xcd8/top753  ebit=777   cut=7
  pc0xcd8/top768  ebit=4967  cut=0
  pc0x30b0/top0   ebit=283   cut=114

TOP 514/515/753/768 survive with only 75 OFFSETs left, so those may be offsets
the game really sets at gameplay rather than desync products. Not checked.

MSCNT CASCADE

18 cut-off lines share mscal 8393 with TOP flipping 0 and 610: MSCNT resumes
the stuck program, and MSCNT also toggles DBF. A cut-off program is resumed
mid-loop rather than restarted, so one runaway costs several budgets.
