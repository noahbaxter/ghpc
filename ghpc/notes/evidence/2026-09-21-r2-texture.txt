R2 step 4: texture on the Vulkan mesh path, and save states that can iterate it
2026-09-21, release, M4 Pro, MoltenVK

Where the texels come from
--------------------------
Not GS VRAM. PsTex keeps its RndBitmap (+0x28) resident in guest RAM for the
life of the texture, because PsTex::LoadVram re-uploads from it whenever the
VRAM allocator evicts. So PsMat::Select reads the bound PsTex's bitmap and
hands it to the scene collector, which decodes it to RGBA8 once per distinct
bitmap. That needs no GS state, cannot race the packet path, and survives
step 5 stubbing the packet path, none of which a VRAM read can say.

Sampled at 90k selects into a driven run: pixels and palette pointers live,
rows linear (rowBytes = w * bpp / 8), palette 0x400 bytes before the pixels at
8bpp and 0x40 at 4bpp, mOrder 3 on every bitmap.

The census, GHPC_TEX_CENSUS=1, read at 0x43f520 (post-patch):

  selects=90000 psm{ 2=6028 19=52444 20=31528 } cpsm{ 0=90000 }
  tfx=84388/2680/2932/0 tcc=90000 csm=0 maxTW=9 maxTH=9

PSMT8 58%, PSMT4 35%, PSMCT16 6%, no CT32. Every palette is CT32, every CSM 0,
every TCC 1.

The decode is proved by looking, GHPC_TEX_DUMP=<dir>: the 8bpp and 4bpp font
atlases come out as crisp glyphs in character order, which a wrong CSM1 index
swizzle or nibble order would scramble. 4bpp is low nibble first.

What arrives, on game_screen (resumed from songmesh):

  [ghpc/scene/tex] afail=6/0/183/0 ateOpaque=0 tfx=487/0/11/0 clamp=42
                   texMissing=0 decoded=182 redecoded=15 renderTarget=0
                   noPixels=3 unsupported=0 oddOrder=0 scaled=0 texa=0/128/0
  [ghpc/vk/mesh]   texMissing=0 textures=167 uploads=182 reuploads=15 texFailed=0

Every textured draw gets its texture. No render-target textures on this
screen, no non-power-of-two bitmaps.

The alpha test
--------------
AFAIL is 183 ZB_ONLY of 189 alpha-tested draws, and every alpha-tested draw
blends. ZB_ONLY means a failed texel writes depth and no colour, so a discard
is exact for colour and loses a depth write the GS would have made. The shader
records which AFAIL cases a discard is exact for.

The punch-out hypothesis was wrong
----------------------------------
The handoff predicted that texture would collapse nvOnly, on the theory that
the alpha-tested draws rendering solid were the native coverage the software
path lacks. Beat-paired, one binary, software reference:

                 bucket 1   bucket 2   bucket 4   bucket 5
  untextured     56424      48680      86156      86412      nvOnly
  textured       56073      46433      84918      85070      nvOnly
  textured again 56073      46433      84918      85070      nvOnly

Texture moved nvOnly by 1-5%. The excess is not the punch-outs. The native
frame shows a floor and a wall covering the view where the software path has
the scoreboard, lamps and fretboard: the large-surface fault the backlog
already names, which texture does not touch.

swOnly rose by 1-2k per bucket with texture. The masks are a brightness
threshold and dark texels now fall under it, so the metric weakens as the
native picture becomes real. Treat swOnly as approximate from here.

Save states carry the mesh cache
--------------------------------
The host mesh cache moved from the PsMesh::Sync override into the runtime
(ghpc_mesh_cache.h) and is written as a MESH chunk. A state without the chunk
still loads and says it will serve no draws.

  capture   GHPC_STATE_SAVE=songmesh GHPC_STATE_SAVE_AT=2.0, fired at
            song=2.016, 135.9 MB (was about 132)
  resume    mesh cache restored, 888 meshes; [ghpc/scene] draws=541,
            against draws=0 before

Resumed against driven, textured, same beat: iou 0.9919 at bucket 4 and 0.9205
at bucket 5. Close, not identical, which is what having no determinism gate
predicts. Fine for A/B as long as every arm loads the same state and one arm
repeats another.

ghpc/scripts/vkarms.sh does that: three arms from one state, four buckets
each, in 2:52 against about 20 minutes for driven arms on a flat timer.

  tex2 vs ref  iou 0.5975 0.5983 0.5934 0.6032
