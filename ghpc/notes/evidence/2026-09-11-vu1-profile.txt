ATTEMPT, SAME: skip commitReadyPipelines when nothing is due

Tracked a pending count and the minimum readyCycle across the seven
pipeline arrays, early-out on `pending == 0 || nextReady > cycle`, recount
after each commit pass. Debug build: zero bookkeeping mismatches over 84,000
MSCALs, zero cut-offs, rung 9. Release, same binary:

    early-out on        15.39 / 15.29 / 14.66 Mcycles/sec
    GHPC_VU1_PIPE_LEGACY 11.41 / 10.93 / 10.95

The legacy arm is not a control: it still pays the recount after every
commit, which is a second full scan, and that is what it lost. Against the
previous binary (14.75, 14.88, 14.66) the early-out arm is unchanged. The
early-out rarely fires: FMAC latency is 4 and the stream issues one upper
per cycle, so one entry becomes ready every cycle and the scan runs anyway.
Reverted.

What would work instead: the scan itself is the cost, not the empty case.
Per-pipeline queues kept in ready order (constant latency per pipeline makes
issue order ready order, except vf writes from loads) with head-pop commits
would make a commit O(ready entries) instead of O(51 slots). Estimated 20 to
30% of VU1 time, so ~1.25x gameplay at best. The seam removes VU1 entirely
and is where the 20x is.
