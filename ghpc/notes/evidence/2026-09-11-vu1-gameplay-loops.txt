PROBE

GHPC_VU1_LOOP=N dumps the first N cut-off runs, which were always menu ones.
GHPC_VU1_LOOP_FROM=<mscal> holds the probe (and its pc histogram) off until the
MSCAL counter passes that point, so the dumps land on game_screen.

RESULT: ALL TEN ARE 0xcd8, AND THEY DO NOT SHARE A LOOP

  dumps 1-3   the zero-header loop, IBNE vi3, vi4 @0x0bc0 (back edge to
              0x0b10). Dump 1 shows the way in: 0x0d30 -> 0x0000 taken once,
              the JALR to header.w = 0. Bound vi4 = -24974.
  dump 4      the loop in the header.w = 0x3c6 routine, IBNE vi4, vi0 @0x2060.
              vi4 is a count from ILW.x vi4, 0(vi3) at 0x1e38, decremented by
              1 at 0x2000, exit at exactly 0. Entered this MSCAL at 892 (a
              resumed run), cut off at 723.
  dumps 5-6   0xcd8's own vertex loop, IBNE vi3, vi12 @0x10a8. vi12 = TOP +
              header.z and vi3 = TOP + header.y are both -31358: TOP 514 and a
              header with y = z = -31872, read from addr 0x2020. The loop
              increments before it tests, so a batch with y == z steps past its
              bound and never meets it.
  dumps 7-10  0x30b0's vertex loop, IBNE vi3, vi12 @0x34c0 (back edge
              0x34d0 -> 0x32b0), running inside an 0xcd8 MSCAL. Headers look
              ordinary here (y 293, z 314 at TOP 0; y 273, z 293 at TOP 514),
              yet the back edges are taken ~800 times while vi3 moves only 15
              to 20, with the unconditional B at 0x3700 and the edges from
              0x3710 and 0x3720 nearly as hot. Not explained.

In 6 of 10 the loop is sound and its count or bound is not: a zero header, a
y == z header of -31872, a strip count near 900 in a 1024-quadword memory. The
code runs away because the input at TOP is bad.

WHERE THE BAD INPUT SITS

At gameplay TOPs. Gameplay never uses TOP 330; its double buffer comes from
OFFSET 514 and 515 (TOP 0, 514, 515), set by OFFSET VIFcodes that carry NUM 2
or 3. With BASE 0 and OFFSET 514 the second input half starts at qw 514, so a
batch over 166 quadwords runs into the constants at 680 to 703 and the output
buffers at 704 and 849. The projection matrix at qw 700 to 703 going bad right
at the start of gameplay ([projw], mscal 8378 to 8570) fits that.

Whether those OFFSETs are real or read out of a payload is not established. The
residual desync still leaves about 1000 invalid opcodes and a handful of dirty
chunks per run, and an OFFSET word like 0x02020203 reads just as well as four
packed bytes.

The TOP split cannot settle it alone: TOP 330 ends 4913 of 4914 runs, but only
on menus, so it is confounded with menus against gameplay.

LOOPS, FROM THE ELF

Every backward branch in the code 0xcd8 can reach was listed statically. The
relevant ones:

  0xcd8 vertex loop    0x0e98-0x10a8, IBNE vi3, vi12
                       vi3 = TOP + header.y (0x0d90, 0x0db0), +1 at 0x1050
                       vi12 = TOP + header.z (0x0db8, 0x0df0)
  zero-header loop     0x0b10-0x0bc0, IBNE vi3, vi4, step 4, exits only for
                       y = 1 (mod 4)
  w=0x3c6 routine      0x1f48-0x2060, IBNE vi4, vi0, count down by 1
  0x14d8 clipper loop  0x15c0-0x17c8, vi5 = 32 doubling to vi1 = 1024. Only
                       re-entered through B 0x1640 after LQ vf31, 4(vi3). Not
                       hot in any dump.
